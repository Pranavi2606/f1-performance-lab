# Data use and responsible publication

The project retrieves historical public race data through OpenF1 and Jolpica. Access conditions and API policies can change; verify published terms before commercial use or republishing cached datasets. Do not infer ownership of race footage or marks from public viewing access.

Videos, extracted frames, controller logs, steering weights and private data stay local and are ignored by Git. Use only video you own or are licensed to process. A computer-vision estimate is **not official F1 steering telemetry**, and it cannot diagnose oversteer from steering-image motion alone.

Optional Ultralytics tooling carries AGPL/commercial licensing considerations; see https://www.ultralytics.com/license. You must decide whether incorporating it into your distributed application is compatible with your intended license. The optional module does not bundle a wheel detector or pretrained checkpoint.

## Attribution policy

Cite the data provider in each chart/export. Keep a record of retrieval time and query parameters. Do not redistribute bulk data unless the provider's current terms permit it. Mark each field as observed, derived, modelled, or unavailable. No proprietary team data is claimed.

## F1 onboard steering-angle research: split provenance requirement

- Dataset: `daniel-saed/F1-steering-angle-dataset`, annotated apparent wheel rotation in onboard imagery, **not official steering telemetry**.
- Imported manifest fields: `video_id`, `frame_index`, `frame_path`, `angle_deg`. **It does not contain verified OpenF1 session keys, FIA driver numbers, or video/lap timestamps.** Do not invent these fields.
- Historical baseline: 1 epoch, random frozen EfficientNet-B0 encoder, filename-ID disjoint split, 3,986 candidate sequences, **source footage overlap not audited**; results are exploratory.
- For audited runs: create `recording_groups_review.csv` using `python -m steering_research.audit_recording_groups --manifest steering_data/f1_steering_manifest.csv`; examine original frame source and fill `source_recording_group` with a common identifier for clips derived from the same original video/lap. Set `review_status=VERIFIED` only when justified by footage/source evidence. Verify with `--check`. Train with `--group-map` to split by this group rather than filename. Do not mix old checkpoints with audited partitions.
- When source `session_key` and `driver_number` genuinely become available, record them with provenance in `verified_source_session` and `verified_driver_number` columns. They are **not prerequisites for a conservative manually audited source group**, and driver/session alone may still fail to distinguish re-edits of the same source; always consider lap/clip overlap.
- Candidate contiguous numeric frame IDs are **not proof of exact frame rate**. Manual steering annotations may exceed ±180°; do not automatically wrap/clip them without checking label conventions.
- Underlying video redistribution/training rights have not been established from the dataset card alone.

## Steering video provenance and split audit — 6 Nov 2026

The first exploratory experiment (one epoch) used 5,553 annotated images, 72 inferred filename groups and a frozen, randomly initialized EfficientNet-B0 encoder. Its reported CNN-GRU-flow improvement and 47.1° test MAE **cannot be described as leakage-cleared**. A subsequent SHA-256 audit found six byte-identical clusters spanning training and validation, all from `antonelli-pov-japon-race` / `antonelli-pov-japon-race-v2`, with annotation disagreement of 4°–15°. The exact-hash audit produced 5,547 unique images from 5,553 entries.

The candidate **3,690-row** subset quarantines the 12 conflicting entries and provisionally removes 1,851 potentially game-rendered `juego` entries. Its 66 filename groups are mapped to 40 **UNREVIEWED** driver/event source families. Preliminary seed-42 group partition: 2,432 training / 431 validation / 827 reserved-test images; zero proposed group crossings. An earlier pHash example report exposes 48 visual-similarity *example rows* crossing the proposed partitions (21 unique pairs); these are not confirmed shared footage and require visual/source-URL verification. This split is **provisional, NOT independently certified**. The metadata lacks trustworthy F1 session key, driver number, source recording URL, exact frame timestamps and laps.

The trainer allows `--allow-provisional-groups --validation-only` to investigate training convergence with explicitly unverified groups. This mode records training loss and validation MAE per epoch, saves the best checkpoint, and **does not compute final-test scores**. Do not claim source-independent accuracy or loss convergence without reviewing the actual evidence. The manual visual labels describe apparent visible wheel rotation; their angle convention and camera-view dependencies need further study. Underlying original F1 footage rights are unresolved; no video frames are packaged for distribution.
